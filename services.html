Services

SEO and Google Ads for businesses that want more enquiries from search, with tracking that shows what's working.

  • SEO audits

    A full check of your website to find what's holding back rankings, with a prioritized list of fixes.

    • Technical crawl: indexing, redirects, broken links, sitemaps
    • Core Web Vitals and page speed review
    • On-page review of titles, headings, meta tags and content
    • Competitor and keyword gap analysis

  • On-page and technical SEO

    Hands-on fixes and ongoing optimization so search engines understand your pages and people find them.

    • Keyword research and mapping to pages
    • Meta tags, headings, alt text and internal links
    • Schema markup for rich results
    • WordPress SEO with Yoast or AIOSEO

  • Google Ads management

    Campaigns set up and managed around leads and sales, not just clicks.

    • Search, Display, YouTube, Performance Max and Demand Gen
    • Keyword, audience and ad copy testing
    • Budget and bidding optimization
    • Regular performance reviews

  • Conversion tracking setup

    Know exactly which pages, keywords and ads bring in enquiries.

    • Google Tag Manager setup
    • GA4 events and conversions
    • Google Ads conversion tracking
    • Form, call and WhatsApp click tracking

  • Reporting dashboards

    Clear Looker Studio reports you can open any time, instead of spreadsheets.

    • Google Ads, GA4 and Search Console in one place
    • Monthly summary of what changed and why
    • Shareable with your team
