About me

I'm an SEO & SEM specialist from Redi, Sindhudurg, Maharashtra, with 8 years of experience helping businesses grow through search.

I started out managing social media and a company WordPress website, which is where I learned how much search traffic depends on the details: page titles, site speed, structured data and the way content answers real questions. Today I run SEO and Google Ads for client websites at Team Inertia Technologies, from audits and keyword strategy to campaign setup, conversion tracking and reporting.

I use AI tools like Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini to work faster on keyword research, content briefs, ad copy and data analysis.
