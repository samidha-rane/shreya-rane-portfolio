What the ads delivered

205 conversions in October 2026, by type.

  • WhatsApp enquiries 172
  • Website bookings 32

The ads also drove 57 clicks-to-call, 195 listing engagements and 6 direction requests.

Keywords and locations

  • “taxi service in goa” brought 121 of October's 205 conversions, converting 24.4% of its clicks.
  • “goa cab car rental” had the best click-through rate in the account, at 14.34%.
  • Madgaon returned the most conversions of any location, and searches about the Dabolim airport area now convert too.

What I did

  • Run the search campaign for taxi bookings, airport transfers and outstation trips.
  • Track website bookings, WhatsApp enquiries and calls from ads as conversions.
  • Prepare a monthly report comparing each month with the last, with keyword, location and budget analysis.
